# Solar calculator

The calculator is a preliminary sizing estimate for residential and small commercial loads.

## How it works

- Users add common appliances, quantity, wattage and daily hours.
- The system estimates daily energy consumption.
- It derives peak load, inverter size, battery capacity and solar array capacity.
- It recommends matching products from the product catalog.

## Important disclaimer

This calculator provides a preliminary estimate based on the information provided. Final system sizing should be confirmed by a qualified solar professional after assessing actual energy usage, installation environment, equipment requirements and site conditions.
